## Context

What is the situation that calls for a decision? What are the forces at play —
technical, organisational, methodological? Be specific. An ADR with a vague
context is useless to future readers.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? Phrase as an active commitment, not a description.

## Alternatives considered

What other shapes were on the table, and why did this one win? An ADR without
this section is incomplete; the decision-versus-alternative trade-off is the
most useful thing future readers extract.

## Consequences

What follows from this decision? What becomes easier? What becomes harder?
What new commitments has the team taken on? Which other decisions does this
constrain or invalidate?

## Reversibility

Is this decision reversible, and at what cost? "Reversible with effort" is a
useful answer. Decisions whose blast radius grows with time should say so.
